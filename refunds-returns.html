Refunds & Returns

Wholesale returns and remedy policy for approved LeafLock™ stockists.

Wholesale goods are supplied in business-to-business transactions. Returns are accepted for faulty or damaged goods, incorrect supply, or short delivery notified within 7 business days of receipt.

Change-of-mind returns are not accepted unless agreed in writing. Opened consumable or mixed products may not be returnable for hygiene and compliance reasons.

Approved returns must be in resalable condition where applicable. LeafLock™ will repair, replace, or credit at its discretion. Refunds for prepaid orders are processed to the original payment method where practicable.

Contact info@leaflock.com.au with your order reference, photos where relevant, and a description of the issue.
