Wholesale Credit Terms Policy

New customer

  • First order: Payment required in full before dispatch (Prepaid).

Returning customer

  • Second order: Maximum order value may be increased to up to double the first order, subject to the first invoice being paid on time.

Credit terms

Third order

  • Eligible to apply for 30-day account terms, provided all previous invoices have been paid in full and on time.

Established customers

  • May apply for 60-day account terms after demonstrating a consistent payment history and satisfactory trading relationship.

Major retail groups, pharmacy chains & distributors

  • 90-day account terms may be offered by approval only.
  • Approval is at the sole discretion of LeafLock™ and may require credit checks, trade references, or additional security.

General conditions

  • All credit terms are subject to approval and may be withdrawn or amended at any time.
  • Overdue accounts may result in the suspension of future orders until payment is received.
  • Ownership of all goods remains with LeafLock™ until invoices have been paid in full.
  • LeafLock™ reserves the right to request payment in advance for any order at its discretion.

Additional conditions

Retention of title Title and ownership in all goods supplied by LeafLock & Co Pty Ltd (trading as LeafLock™) remain with LeafLock™ until all amounts owing in respect of those goods (and any other amounts owing by the customer) have been paid in full.

Late payment If an invoice becomes seriously overdue, LeafLock™ may charge interest on the overdue amount at the rate permitted under the Interest on Overdue Payments (Suppliers) Act 1996 (Qld) or other applicable Australian law, and may recover reasonable costs of collection and enforcement.

Personal guarantee Where LeafLock™ extends credit above standard prepaid terms, a director or authorised officer of the customer company may be required to provide a personal guarantee of payment if the company fails to pay amounts when due.

Suspension of supply LeafLock™ may suspend or cancel supply, withhold dispatch, or require prepayment for any future order if any invoice is overdue or if LeafLock™ reasonably believes the customer’s credit position has deteriorated.
